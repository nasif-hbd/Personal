Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

The short version. Mindora stores your study plans on your own device. There is no account, no tracking, no analytics, and no advertising. Two optional features send data somewhere else, and both are described below.

What is stored on your device

Everything you create — your study plans, lesson progress, watch history, completed items and settings — is stored locally in your browser or app storage. It never leaves your device unless you turn on one of the optional features below, or export it yourself.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser data, deletes it permanently. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracker in this app. We do not create user profiles, and we do not sell or share data with anyone, because we do not have any.

Optional: AI planning features

If you use the AI to build or edit a study plan, the text of your request and the plan being edited are sent to Anthropic (the Claude API) to generate a response. This happens only when you send a message.

Where the app is configured to use a shared server, that request passes through that server, which forwards it to Anthropic. The server records only anonymous usage counters — how many requests came from a browser, so that rate limits can be enforced. Those counters are not linked to any identity, and message content is not stored on the server.

Anthropic's handling of the data it receives is governed by its own privacy policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

If you never use the AI features, nothing is ever sent.

Optional: Google Drive sync

If you connect Google Drive, the app saves a single backup file containing your plans to your own Google Drive. The app requests the narrowest permission Google offers (drive.file), which grants access only to the one file the app itself creates. It cannot see, read, or modify anything else in your Drive.

That file is in your Drive, under your control. Delete it at any time, or disconnect from the app's Settings screen to revoke access.

This feature is unavailable in the Android and iOS apps, because Google does not permit its sign-in flow inside an app's embedded browser.

Video content

Lessons play through embedded YouTube players. When a video loads, YouTube receives the request directly and applies its own privacy practices, including any cookies it sets. Mindora does not send YouTube any information about you.

YouTube's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Mindora is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for us to delete on request — but if you have a concern, contact us at the address below.

Data deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you delete it by deleting the app or clearing its storage. To delete a Drive backup, remove the file from your Google Drive or disconnect Drive in Settings. Nothing is retained anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the updated version is published at this same address.
