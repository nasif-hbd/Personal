Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

The short version. Your study plans, progress and settings stay on your own device — there is no account and no tracking. A handful of features are opt-in and send a specific, limited piece of data somewhere else so the feature can work: joining the leaderboard publishes a display name you choose, sending feedback sends your message, claiming a paid plan sends the transaction reference so a human can verify it. Each is described exactly below, with what's sent and who can see it.

What is stored on your device

Everything you create — your study plans, lesson progress, watch history, completed items, notes, flashcards and settings — is stored locally in your browser or app storage. It never leaves your device unless you turn on one of the optional features below, or export it yourself.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser data, deletes it permanently. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had it.

What we don't collect

We don't run analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers, and we don't build profiles or sell data — we have none to sell. We don't ask for your real name, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and nothing on your device is uniquely fingerprinted for tracking.

A few features described below do send a specific, limited piece of information — a display name you type in, a feedback message, a payment reference — because that's what the feature does. Those are opt-in and covered individually; nothing is collected passively in the background.

Optional: AI planning features

If you use the AI to build or edit a study plan, or to generate a quiz for a lesson, the text of your request and the relevant plan content are sent to Anthropic (the Claude API) to generate a response. This happens only when you send a message.

Where the app is configured to use a shared server, that request passes through that server, which forwards it to Anthropic. The server records only anonymous usage counters — how many requests came from a browser, so that rate limits can be enforced. Those counters are not linked to any identity, and message content is not stored on the server.

The assistant is instructed to stay focused on your study plan and study-skills questions, and to decline requests that try to redirect it elsewhere — that's a behavioral instruction given to the model, not a data-handling change, and it doesn't affect what's sent or stored.

Anthropic's handling of the data it receives is governed by its own privacy policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

If you never use the AI features, nothing is ever sent.

Certificates

When you finish a plan, Mindora can generate a certificate. The name printed on it is whatever you type into the name field — it is stored only on your device, the same as the rest of your plan data, and is never sent to any server. Downloading or printing the certificate is something your browser does locally; Mindora doesn't see or keep a copy.

Optional: Leaderboard Opt-in

The leaderboard is off by default. If you choose to join it, three things happen:

  • The display name you type in — not your account or real name, since there is no account — along with your XP, level, lesson count and streak, is sent to the server and shown publicly on the board to anyone who opens it.
  • These figures are self-reported: they come from your device's own record of your progress, the same numbers shown on your own Awards screen. Mindora cannot verify they're accurate, and says so on the board itself.
  • A random visitor token (not tied to your identity) is used so the server knows which row is yours when you come back — it is not a login and carries no personal information.

You can leave the leaderboard at any time from the Awards screen; this removes your row and the server stops keeping it. Joining again assigns a new row.

Optional: Feedback Opt-in

The feedback panel is only used if you open it and send a message. When you do, the message text, the category you picked (bug, idea, etc.), and — only if you choose to fill it in — an optional contact address are sent to the server. A little context about what screen you were on is attached automatically, so the developer doesn't have to ask.

That message is stored so it isn't lost, and the server attempts to email it to the app's developer. If email delivery is misconfigured or down, the message still waits safely in storage rather than being dropped.

Feedback is not visible to other visitors, only to the developer. There is a small daily limit per visitor so the inbox can't be flooded. If you'd like a feedback message removed, contact us at the address below.

Optional: Payments & subscriptions Opt-in

Mindora does not process card payments and never sees your card number, bank login, or mobile-wallet PIN. Subscribing works like this: you send money through an external payment method you already use (for example bKash, Nagad, or a bank transfer) exactly as you normally would, entirely outside the app, and then tell Mindora you did.

What you tell Mindora — the plan you're claiming, the payment method, the amount, and the transaction reference number from your payment confirmation, plus an optional sender name or number if you provide one — is sent to the server and stored so a human can manually check it against the real transaction and unlock your subscription. This is the same transaction ID your payment app already shows you; nothing more sensitive is requested or stored.

Payment records are visible only to the app's developer, for verification, never to other visitors.

Optional: Google Drive sync Opt-in

If you connect Google Drive, the app saves a single backup file containing your plans to your own Google Drive. The app requests the narrowest permission Google offers (drive.file), which grants access only to the one file the app itself creates. It cannot see, read, or modify anything else in your Drive.

That file is in your Drive, under your control. Delete it at any time, or disconnect from the app's Settings screen to revoke access.

This feature is unavailable in the Android and iOS apps, because Google does not permit its sign-in flow inside an app's embedded browser.

Video content

Lessons play through embedded YouTube players. When a video loads, YouTube receives the request directly and applies its own privacy practices, including any cookies it sets. Mindora does not send YouTube any information about you.

When a lesson has no specific video attached, the app can ask its server to look one up by searching YouTube on your behalf. That search query (the lesson title and subject) is sent to the server, which relays it to YouTube using the app's own API key — never yours — and caches the result so the same lookup isn't repeated. No personal information is included in that request.

YouTube's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children & teens

Mindora is built to be usable by learners of many ages, including high-school students, and is not directed specifically at children under 13. The app itself doesn't ask anyone's age.

Everything in the sections above applies the same way regardless of age: the leaderboard display name, a feedback message, or a payment reference is only ever sent if that specific feature is used, and it's exactly what a parent could review by looking at the feature itself. We'd encourage a parent or guardian to be aware of a younger student using the optional leaderboard, since a chosen display name is shown publicly — nothing else on the board is.

If you're a parent or guardian with a concern about a specific piece of data, contact us at the address below and we'll act on it.

Data deletion

  • On-device data (plans, progress, certificates, settings): delete the app or clear its site storage. This is permanent and immediate — we never had a copy.
  • Leaderboard entry: use "Leave the leaderboard" on the Awards screen at any time.
  • Google Drive backup: delete the file from your Drive, or disconnect in Settings.
  • A feedback message or payment record already sent: these aren't self-service, since there's no account to log into — contact us at the address below and we'll remove it.
